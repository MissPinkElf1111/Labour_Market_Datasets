# Labour_Market_Datasets
1. ***Job Vacancies and Vacancy Rates***<br>
Statistics Canada · 14-10-0432-01 · Monthly<br>
Contains the number of job vacancies, payroll employees, and job vacancy rates by province and territory, seasonally adjusted.<br>
Role: Prediction target<br>
Use: Prediction target(Y), especially for future job vacancy rates or job vacancy counts.<br>
https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1410043201<br>

2. ***Labour Force Characteristics***<br>
Statistics Canada · 14-10-0287-01 · Monthly<br>
Contains unemployment rate, employment rate, participation rate, labour force size, and other labour indicators, with age and gender breakdowns.<br>
Role: ML predictor<br>
Use: Main explanatory variables (X).<br>
https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1410028701<br>

3. ***Employment and Average Weekly Earnings***<br>
Statistics Canada · 14-10-0223-01 · Monthly<br>
Contains average weekly earnings and employment estimates by province and industry.<br>
Role: ML predictor<br>
Use: Wage and employment-related explanatory variables.<br>
https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1410022301<br>

4. ***Job Vacancies and Offered Wages by Occupation***<br>
Statistics Canada · 14-10-0444-01 · Quarterly<br>
Contains job vacancies and average offered hourly wages by occupation and geography.<br>
Role: Additional analysis
Use: Optional extension to examine which types of jobs experience greater demand and whether offered wages improve predictions.<br>
https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1410044401<br>

5. ***Average offered hourly wage***<br>
Statistics Canada · 14-10-0442-01  · Quarterly<br>
Contains job vacancies, job vacancy rates, payroll employees, and average offered hourly wages by industry subsector and geography.<br>
Role: Industry comparison<br>
Use: Compare labour demand across industries and Canadian provinces, identify industries experiencing higher vacancy rates, and examine relationships between offered wages and job vacancies.<br>
https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1410044201<br>
