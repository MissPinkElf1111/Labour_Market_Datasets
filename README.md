# Labour_Market_Datasets
1. ***Job Vacancies and Vacancy Rates***<br>
Statistics Canada · 14-10-0432-01 · Monthly<br>
Contains the number of job vacancies, payroll employees, and job vacancy rates by province and territory, seasonally adjusted.<br>
Role: Prediction target<br>
Use: Your prediction target (Y), specifically future job vacancy rates or job vacancy counts.<br>
https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1410043201<br>

2. Labour Force Characteristics
Statistics Canada · 14-10-0287-01 · Monthly
Contains unemployment rate, employment rate, participation rate, labour force size, and other labour indicators, with age and gender breakdowns.
Role: ML predictor
Use: Main explanatory variables (X).
https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1410028701

3. Employment and Average Weekly Earnings
Statistics Canada · 14-10-0223-01 · Monthly
Contains average weekly earnings and employment estimates by province and industry.
Role: ML predictor
Use: Wage and employment-related explanatory variables.
https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1410022301

4. Job Vacancies and Offered Wages by Occupation
Statistics Canada · 14-10-0444-01 · Quarterly
Contains job vacancies and average offered hourly wages by occupation and geography.
Role: Additional analysis
Use: Optional extension to examine which types of jobs experience greater demand and whether offered wages improve predictions.
https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1410044401

5. Average offered hourly wage
Statistics Canada · 14-10-0442-01  · Quarterly
Contains job vacancies, job vacancy rates, payroll employees, and average offered hourly wages by industry subsector and geography.
Role: Industry comparison
Use: Compare labour demand across industries and Canadian provinces, identify industries experiencing higher vacancy rates, and examine relationships between offered wages and job vacancies.
https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1410044201
